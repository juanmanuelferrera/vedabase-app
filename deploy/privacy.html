Privacy Policy

Overview

Vedabase Original is a desktop application that provides offline access to the original works of A. C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

Data Collection

We do not collect any personal data.

Vedabase Original is designed to work entirely offline. The application:

  • Does not require user registration or login
  • Does not collect personal information
  • Does not track your reading habits or usage patterns
  • Does not use analytics or tracking services
  • Does not transmit any data to external servers

Local Storage

The application stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Personal notes: Any annotations you create are stored only on your device
  • Reading preferences: Settings like font size and dark mode are saved locally

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

Internet Connection

Vedabase Original does not require an internet connection to function. All content is embedded within the application. No data is sent or received over the network.

Third-Party Services

This application does not integrate with any third-party services, advertising networks, or analytics platforms.

Open Source

Vedabase Original is open source software. You can review the complete source code on GitHub to verify these privacy practices.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through our website at vedabase.bhaktiyoga.es.
